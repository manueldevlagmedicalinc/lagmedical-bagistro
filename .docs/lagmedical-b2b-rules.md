# Lag Medical B2B Rules

## Company Overview

Lag Medical Inc is a B2B ophthalmic equipment, optical products, and medical technology distribution company.

The platform serves:

* Optical stores
* Optical laboratories
* Ophthalmologists
* Optometrists
* Eye clinics
* Medical distributors
* Procurement departments
* International resellers

The platform is not a retail ecommerce store.

---

## Business Model

Lag Medical operates as a procurement and distribution platform.

The objective is not to maximize online transactions but to facilitate:

* Product discovery
* Product comparison
* Quotation requests
* Procurement workflows
* Supplier relationships
* International sourcing
* B2B lead generation

Many transactions continue outside the platform through sales representatives, quotations, purchase orders, financing agreements, or distributor negotiations.

---

## Pricing Rules

Pricing visibility may vary by:

* Customer group
* Customer account type
* Sales channel
* Product category
* Supplier requirements

Never assume all products display pricing.

Never assume checkout is enabled.

Never assume customers can purchase directly online.

Some products may require:

* Request for quotation (RFQ)
* Contact sales
* Purchase order workflow
* Distributor authorization
* Financing approval

## Content Guidelines

Content must be written for professionals.

Focus on:

* Technical specifications
* Clinical applications
* Business benefits
* Operational efficiency
* Reliability
* Compliance
* Product support

Avoid:

* Consumer-focused language
* Retail sales language
* Emotional marketing
* Promotional hype
* Aggressive sales tactics

---

## Procurement Mindset

When generating recommendations, workflows, or UX proposals:

Prioritize:

1. Trust
2. Product information quality
3. Supplier credibility
4. Technical documentation
5. Procurement efficiency
6. Lead generation
7. Long-term relationships

Do not optimize solely for immediate online conversion.

## Decision Rule

If a recommendation would be suitable for a typical retail ecommerce store but conflicts with a professional B2B procurement workflow, always prioritize the B2B procurement approach.
