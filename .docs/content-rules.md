# Content Rules

## Audience

Write for:

- Clinics
- Laboratories
- Optical stores
- Distributors
- Procurement teams

Never assume retail customers.

## Tone

Use:

- Professional
- Technical
- Corporate
- Informative

Avoid:

- Hype
- Clickbait
- Consumer marketing

## Product Content

Focus on:

- Specifications
- Applications
- Compatibility
- Workflow benefits

Avoid:

- Emotional selling
- Aggressive calls to action

## Writing Style

Prefer:

- Short paragraphs
- Clear language
- Technical accuracy

Avoid:

- Marketing fluff
